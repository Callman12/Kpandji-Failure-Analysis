# Speaker Notes & Defense Guide: Kpandji Case Study

## Overview for Jury Presentation (IIT L3 Software Engineering)

This guide accompanies the 14-slide professional presentation **« Kpandji: The Rise and Fall of an Ivorian Automotive Dream »**.

### Key Arguments to Master
1. **Semantics & Assembly Model (SKD/CKD):** Acknowledge that Kpandji was an assembly initiative rather than 100% ground-up manufacturing. Emphasize that in industrial economics, this is the standard infant-industry starting point (similar to early South Korean or Chinese models).
2. **The Hardware vs. Software Trap:** Unlike software where iteration is nearly zero-cost, automotive manufacturing has immense capital intensity, stringent supply chain dependencies, and severe unit economics at low volume.
3. **Ecosystem Deficiency:** A car cannot be built in isolation. The lack of local tier-1 suppliers, specialized tooling labs, and technical vocational clusters choked the initiative.
4. **The "Fail Fast, Learn Faster" Takeaway:** Frame Kpandji not as a total failure, but as a necessary pioneer ("sacrificial lamb") that provides invaluable empirical data for future West African tech and engineering entrepreneurs.
